# Lincoln Text Adventure &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Day 3 of 3

**A team of 8th graders builds a playable text adventure game set in Abraham Lincoln's world.**

---

### Today: Build and play-test

Programmers: build what's on your scene map from yesterday. That map is your spec — don't invent new scenes mid-build. Finish what you planned first.

Writers: your job today is to draft the actual words the player will read in each scene. Keep it tight — two or three sentences per scene. Make it vivid. Use what the research team found.

About halfway through the period, stop everything and play-test. Hand the game to someone who wasn't writing the code and watch them play. Don't help them. Just watch and take notes. Then fix what's actually broken — not what you wish you'd designed differently.
