### Today: Research and design

Today your team splits. Some of you will go deep on Lincoln — finding the real facts and details that will make your game feel true. The rest of you will design the game itself: the scenes, the choices, the branches, the endings. At the end of the period the two groups come back together and the researchers brief the designers. You may need to adjust your design based on what the research team found.
