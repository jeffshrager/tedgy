### Today: Getting organized

Before you can build anything, you need to know who's doing what — and have a first idea of what you're actually making. Today your team will figure out what roles are needed, decide who takes each one, and land on a starting idea for your game. By the end of the period you should feel like a team with a plan, not just a group with an assignment.

There's no computer work today. Talk first.

---
